Daily 7 Minutes

A gentle, wrist-friendly workout. Everything is done standing or in a chair — no weight on your hands or wrists (where you use the wall, only your elbows touch it), and no getting down on the floor.

What you'll need

A sturdy chair without wheels, a clear spot of wall, and the bottom step of a staircase (or any low, stable step).

Today's 12 exercises

30 seconds each, with 10 seconds to rest in between. Strength moves are done slowly — about 3 seconds up and 3 seconds down. Moving slowly is what builds muscle.

When a move starts to feel easy, try its "Harder" version. Making things a little harder over time is how muscle keeps growing.

    Please remember

    Move slowly and stay within a comfortable range. Mild effort is good; sharp pain is not — stop that exercise and rest until the next one. Breathe normally and don't hold your breath. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise routine.
